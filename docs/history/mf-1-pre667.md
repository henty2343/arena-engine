### The spin ceiling measured — `omegaCap` 140 is dormant, the real ceiling is structural, and the sim is stable to ω ≈ 258 (2026-10-28, owner pick "measure the real limit first" out of four offered options; read-only, no code changed, canonical byte-identical before and after at `54ab0e5b784962987fdec6cc71cd6636`).
The owner met the item with "what is this ? i dont understand", so the finding is stated plainly: every damaging hit spins the winner faster, nothing in the _game_ limits that, but the code carries a hard stop at 140 rad/s purely so the physics cannot tunnel — and the question was how close the game actually gets to it.
**Method (three read-only measurements).** (1) _Analytic:_ the sim's own substep-sizing rule, `k = ceil((subMargin + Σ(|vel| + ω·REACH))·dt / δ)` with δ = (MINR[a]+MINR[b])/2, clamped to `maxSub` 512 and dt = 1/120, solved per pair for the ω at which required k reaches 512 — all 484 ordered pairs. (2) _Empirical:_ a staged copy with `omegaCap: 100000` (`.qa/.uncapped.html`, deleted afterwards; the canonical was never edited for this) run through natscan and projectile-fix and diffed against the capped runs.
(3) _Structural:_ per-round accounting of hits, damage and spin on the seeds that peak. **Finding A — the clamp never engages.** With the clamp removed, natscan is **identical on 30/30 seeds** (total ticks 891,003 on both sides, no-winner naturals 33 and 36 on both sides, every maxSpin unchanged) and projectile-fix is **identical across all 18 forced cells** (A 0, B 0/11893 launches off doctrine, C **0/320771** budget-under-provisioned ticks, D 9/18). Nothing in the shipped game reaches 140 rad/s. **Finding B — why: the ceiling is HP, not the clamp.** A round ends when hp 100 is drained, which at base damage 1 is exactly **100 damaging hits**; the fastest spin axis is +1.0/hit (launcher ability 0.70 + Speedy 0.30), so the structural maximum is 3.5 + 100×1.0 = **103.5 rad/s** — precisely the observed peak, on 9 of 30 naturals (seeds 13, 15, 30, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 40).
Verified per round: the peak rounds are pure-projectile (blade hits [0,0]) with the spinner's maxDmg pinned at 1.00 (Speedy + a launcher never grows damage) while its heavy opponent grew to 10.9–20.2. Reaching 140 needs **137 hits**, i.e.
hp ÷ damage above 137. Melee weapons cannot get close: their ability spin is 0.0, so Speedy + melee tops out at 3.5 + 100×0.30 = 33.5. **Finding C — the true stability limit.** Blade-budget saturation (required k = maxSub 512) across the 484 pairs: worst **axe+axe at ω 257.8** (δ 1.80, REACH sum 424.3), then axe+flail / flail+axe 276.0, hammer+axe / axe+hammer 278.6, axe+spear 288.0; p10 323.8, **median 424.6**, p90 765.8, loosest orb+orb 4346.5 (MINR 21.8 — a sphere has no thin circle). **No pair saturates below ω 140.** The projectile budget at ω 140 peaks at k = 63.3 (a bow shooting at an axe-wielding foe) = **12.4% of maxSub**, so blades bind, not shots.
Body velocity is second-order (the ω·REACH term dominates, and `subMargin` 1200 already covers knock/push/bounce impulses); `CFG.cruise` no longer exists — the movement doctrine was reverted 2026-10-21 — and `maxSpeed` is 1200. **Reading of the three findings together.** The 36.5-hit headroom figure recorded earlier the same day was arithmetically right but framed wrong: the binding constraint is hp ÷ damage per hit, and the clamp is _conservative_ rather than tight — it bites at 54% of the worst pair's stability limit and about a third of the median's.
